About Geocymatics

Geocymatics is the study of visible sound and vibration patterns on geological substrates. This involves examining how sound waves interact with different types of rocks, creating intricate and mesmerizing patterns. By studying these patterns, researchers can gain insights into the vibrational behavior and material properties of rocks.
